# Cocoa Beach Two Bedroom Condos in 2026: Why Smaller Is Smart on the Space Coast

Cocoa Beach has beach access, a busy cruise port next door, and a growing aerospace job base. A two bedroom condo is one of the most practical ways to buy into it.

At today's 30 year mortgage rate of 7.28% (Freddie Mac, October 1, 2026), purchase price drives the monthly budget, and condos offer the widest range of entry points in town. This guide covers what two bedroom condos cost to finance, how the Cocoa Beach market is moving, and what is supporting demand on the Space Coast.

## A Smaller Price Tag Keeps the Monthly Payment Comfortable

Here is the monthly principal and interest on a 30 year fixed loan at 7.28% with 20% down. These are my own calculations.

| Purchase price | Monthly payment |
|---|---|
| $249,000 | $1,363 |
| $360,000 | $1,971 |
| $513,493 | $2,811 |

* A condo around $360,000 costs about $840 less per month to finance than a property at the citywide median of $513,493.
* Over a year, that difference is roughly $10,000.
* Moving down to $249,000, the recent median for non waterfront condos and townhouses, lowers the payment further.

Property tax, insurance, and HOA dues come on top of these numbers.

## A Market With Momentum and Room to Negotiate

According to [Redfin's Cocoa Beach data](https://www.redfin.com/city/3436/FL/Cocoa-Beach/housing-market) for July 2026:

* Homes sold: 125, up 38.7% from a year earlier.
* Median days on market: 78, which is 9 days faster than last year.
* Median sale price: $513,493, up 19.6%, which includes higher priced houses.
* Price per square foot: $340 over the last three months, down 2.9%.
* Sale to list price ratio: 96.4%.
* Listings with a price drop: 34.8%.

Sales are active and homes are moving faster. For buyers, the 96.4% ratio and the share of price cuts show real room to negotiate.

## Condos Offer the Most Choice

A local [MLS based market report](https://www.viewbrevardhomes.com/blog/cocoa-beach-real-estate-market-report-updated-february-2026/) from a Brevard brokerage breaks Cocoa Beach down by property type:

* **Median sold price, January 10 to May 28, 2026:** $382,500 for condos and townhouses (128 closings) versus $787,000 for single family houses (51 closings).
* **Median sold price, June 29 to September 27, 2026:** $249,000 for non waterfront condos and townhouses and $516,250 for oceanfront ones (40 closings).
* **Active listings on September 27:** 124 condos and townhouses versus 40 houses.

By my math, about three of every four active listings are condos or townhouses. That gives buyers a wide spread of options, from non waterfront units near $249,000 to oceanfront units above $500,000. The non waterfront figure comes from a small sample of 15 sales, so use it as a rough guide.

## What Is Supporting Demand

Several local trends point in the same direction.

* **Cruise traffic.** [Port Canaveral](https://www.portcanaveral.com/media-center/latest-news/blog/2025/12/02/port-canaveral-officially-world-s-busiest-cruise-port) handled over 8.6 million revenue passenger movements in fiscal year 2025, a record and a 13% increase over 2024. About 75% of passengers drive to the port, which feeds local visitor demand.
* **Launches.** The Space Coast logged a record 109 launches in 2025, per Orlando Sentinel counts, and launch activity has continued in 2026.
* **Aerospace jobs.** A Blue Origin facility announced in May is expected to support 500 jobs averaging above $98,000. A $140 million Lockheed Martin plant in Titusville is projected to add up to 300 jobs averaging $89,000. These are reported announcements, not completed hires.
* **Population.** Census Bureau estimates put Brevard County at 663,982 residents in July 2025, up 9.5% from the April 2020 base of 606,616.

Prices reflect that appeal. The county's median owner occupied home value is $339,600 (2020 to 2024 Census data), while Cocoa Beach's median sale price is about 50% higher, a premium for a beachside address.

## How to Buy a Condo With Confidence

Florida's condo rules now give buyers more documentation than they used to have. A short checklist:

* **Reserve study and inspection.** Buildings three stories or taller must complete milestone inspections and a Structural Integrity Reserve Study. Ask for both, plus any pending or planned special assessments.
* **What the dues cover.** Ask whether the master policy covers insurance and which services are included.
* **Lender eligibility.** Since August 3, 2026, Fannie Mae no longer offers its Limited Review for condos and Freddie Mac retired its Streamlined Review. Ask your lender early whether the building qualifies.
* **Your own insurance quote.** Get a quote for the specific unit, including flood coverage if the building is near the water.
* **Make an offer with room built in.** With 34.8% of listings cutting prices and a typical sale closing near 96.4% of list, offers below asking are common.

## The Bottom Line

Cocoa Beach pairs a beach lifestyle with steady demand from cruising, aerospace, and population growth. Two bedroom condos give buyers a way in at a payment well below the citywide median, with plenty of listings to compare. Read the reserve study, price the full monthly cost, and compare units side by side to find the one that fits.
